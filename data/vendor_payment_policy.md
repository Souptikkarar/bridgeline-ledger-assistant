# Vendor Payment & Procurement Policy
### Bridgeline Fabricators Pvt. Ltd. — Effective 1 April 2024

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs how Bridgeline Fabricators settles supplier invoices, how payment
terms are assigned, and how disputes and credit notes are handled. It applies to all
purchases recorded in the accounts payable ledger for financial year 2024-25.

## 2. Financial year and reporting quarters

Bridgeline follows the Indian financial year.

| Quarter | Period |
|---|---|
| Q1 | 1 April – 30 June |
| Q2 | 1 July – 30 September |
| Q3 | 1 October – 31 December |
| Q4 | 1 January – 31 March |

FY 2024-25 therefore runs from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025. Any invoice dated outside
this window is out of scope for FY 2024-25 reporting and must be excluded from
year-level totals.

## 3. Payment terms by category

Payment terms are assigned by procurement category, not by vendor. The clock starts on
the **invoice date**, not the date the invoice was received or approved.

| Category | Standard terms | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Raw Material | Net 30 | Steel and plate suppliers. No extension permitted. |
| Fabrication | Net 45 | Reflects site milestone certification lag. |
| Transport | Net 15 | Freight vendors are paid on short cycle. |
| Consumables | Net 30 | |
| Tooling | Net 30 | |
| Safety | Net 30 | Safety spend is never deferred on cash-flow grounds. |
| Services | Net 45 | Design, review and consultancy engagements. |

An invoice is **overdue** if, as at the reporting date, it remains unpaid and the number
of days elapsed since the invoice date exceeds the standard terms for its category.

An invoice that has been paid is assessed for **payment delay**, defined as:

> payment delay (days) = payment date − invoice date − standard term days

A negative payment delay means the invoice was settled early. Early settlement is not a
breach and should be reported as a negative number, not clipped to zero.

## 4. Foreign currency purchases

A small number of imported purchases are billed in USD. These are recorded in the ledger
in their original currency. **Foreign currency lines must not be added to rupee totals
without conversion.** For all FY 2024-25 reporting, use a fixed internal rate of
**1 USD = ₹83.50**. This rate is set annually by the finance head and does not float.

## 5. Credit notes

Credit notes are raised against a vendor when material is short-supplied, rejected on
inspection, or returned. They are recorded in the same ledger as invoices, carry a
`CN-` prefix, and are stored as **negative amounts**.

Credit notes reduce the total payable to that vendor and **must be included** in any
vendor-level spend total. A credit note has no payment due date and is never counted as
overdue.

## 6. Duplicate and disputed entries

Where the same invoice number appears more than once in the ledger, the entry with the
**later or revised description** supersedes the earlier one. The superseded line must be
excluded from totals. Duplicates are a known data-entry issue arising from the manual
re-keying of supplier PDFs and are not evidence of vendor fraud.

## 7. GST

GST is charged at the rate stated on the invoice. Expected GST amount is the taxable
amount multiplied by the stated rate. Where the recorded GST amount does not reconcile
to the stated rate within ₹1, the line must be flagged for the accounts team and
excluded from input tax credit claims until corrected.

A supplier GSTIN is mandatory for input tax credit. Invoices carrying no GSTIN cannot be
claimed and must be listed separately each quarter for follow-up.

## 8. Data quality standards

The following conditions are treated as data quality exceptions and must be surfaced in
any report, not silently corrected:

- An invoice dated in the future relative to the reporting date.
- A payment date earlier than the invoice date.
- A missing or blank taxable amount.
- A missing supplier GSTIN.
- The same supplier appearing under more than one spelling.

## 9. Reporting date

Unless otherwise stated, all ageing, overdue and delay calculations for FY 2024-25 are
performed as at **31 March 2025**.
